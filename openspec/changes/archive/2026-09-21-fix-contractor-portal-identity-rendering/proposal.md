# Proposal

## Why

Authorized customer pages fail when QWeb follows an independent Contractor's
protected partner record merely to render the Contractor name.

## What Changes

- Provide a minimal identity projection for Contractors already referenced by an authorized portal document.
- Remove protected Contractor-partner traversal from generic Contract, Proposal, Project, and message rendering.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `contractor-portal-workflow`: Render authorized Contractor identity safely.
